About Me

Just a kid from Point Cook who likes building things with code

I'm Samrath "Sami" Singh, a motivated junior developer from Point Cook, VIC. I have hands-on experience in coding, system design, and client support. At age 14, I registered my own ABN and launched my first business. Since then, I've been building ICT solutions that make a difference.

I create user-focused web applications and digital solutions. I design 3D models for 3DPrintzAU, build custom Minecraft plugins, and develop web apps through Procoders Coding Service. I approach every project with attention to detail and a commitment to quality.

I gather requirements carefully, test thoroughly, and document clearly. I build systems that are functional, secure, well-documented, and genuinely solve problems for users.

I'm currently in Year 9 (age 15) at Point Cook College while running multiple businesses and taking on freelance development work. Next year, I'll be enrolling at Homestead Senior Secondary College for Year 10 to pursue my passion for technology and practical skills. I'm passionate about continuous learning and contributing to impactful projects.

Frequently Asked Questions

A few honest answers about how I work.

💼 What services do you offer?

I specialize in custom web development, Minecraft plugin development, eCommerce solutions (especially Shopify), API integration, and automation. I also provide technical support and troubleshooting services.

⏱️ How long does a typical project take?

Project timelines vary based on complexity and scope. Simple websites can take 1-2 weeks, while custom applications may take 4-8 weeks. I provide detailed timelines during the initial consultation and keep you updated throughout the project.

💰 How much do you charge?

Pricing depends on project scope, complexity, and requirements. I offer both hourly rates and project-based pricing. After understanding your needs, I'll provide a detailed quote with no hidden fees. Contact me for a free consultation and estimate.

🔄 Do you provide ongoing support?

Yes! I offer post-launch support and maintenance packages. This includes bug fixes, updates, and technical assistance. We can discuss ongoing support options that fit your needs and budget.

📍 Do you work with clients outside Australia?

Absolutely! While I'm based in Point Cook, VIC, Australia, I work with clients globally. I'm comfortable with remote collaboration and have experience coordinating across different time zones.

🛠️ What technologies do you work with?

I work with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python, PHP, REST APIs, Cloudflare Workers, and various frameworks. For a complete list, check out my Skills page. I'm always learning new technologies to better serve my clients.

📝 How do we get started?

Simply reach out via the Contact page or email me at samisingh988@gmail.com. We'll schedule a consultation to discuss your project requirements, timeline, and budget. From there, I'll provide a detailed proposal and we can begin!

🎓 Are you still a student?

Yes, I'm currently studying at Point Cook College while running multiple businesses and freelance development work. This doesn't affect my commitment to quality—I've successfully delivered numerous projects and maintain professional standards in all my work.
